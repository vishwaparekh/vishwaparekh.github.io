What is Operational Intelligence?

Building AI is only the beginning.

When AI enters the real world, it encounters changing patients, users, machines, environments, infrastructure — and other AI systems.

Operational Intelligence is the science of making AI work, adapt, and evolve in this changing world.

We study the computational problems that begin when AI leaves the lab.

What keeps us up at night these days?

What happens when AI fails?

One problem at a time. One question we cannot stop thinking about.
